The game

Open squares to locate the bombs hidden around the board. Numbered squares tell you how many neighbouring cells contain a bomb; uncovering one costs a life. Later levels add more bombs.[2]

Development

Eric Thommerot programmed and produced the ExEn game. Axel Bertrand created its artwork, and Emmanuel Cacouault supplied the music as well as beta testing.[3]
