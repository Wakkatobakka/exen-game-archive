The game

Turning the ball into a bubble allows it to cross gaps, but touching a wall bursts it. The game has 25 levels across three environments, with shops selling bonuses and power-ups.[2]

Development

Martial Bossard returned on programming and level design. Kalila Taylor and Julie Morana joined the level-design team; Lionel Gischler and Axel Bertrand supplied the graphics.[3]
