The game

Two worms make up each team. Players take turns moving and firing across randomly generated battlefields, with four types of terrain.[2]

Development

Martial Bossard is credited with programming the 2005 ExEn edition. The catalogue also names Kiloo as the developer of the mobile game.[3][1]
