The game

Compete for championship points while steering around the circuit and managing fuel. Changes in the weather call for different tyres and settings.[2]

Development

Yannick De Pins designed the game, Vincent Duboisdendien programmed it, and Eric Thommerot contributed additional programming. Xavier Carbonnaux produced the project for In-Fusio.[3]
