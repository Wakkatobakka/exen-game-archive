The game

The bike accelerates automatically; the player steers and brakes. Quick Race offers a single event, Time Attack focuses on lap times, and Championship strings races together against opponents.[2]

Development

Frédéric Nespoulous is credited with programming the 2005 ExEn edition.[3]

People

Frédéric Nespoulous

Programming[3]

Release and reception

THQ Wireless published the mobile game. The J2ME release dates to 2003; the ExEn edition followed in 2005.[1]
