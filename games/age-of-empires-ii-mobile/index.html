The game

Age of Empires II Mobile adapts The Age of Kings as a real-time strategy game. Players build a settlement, manage its resources and command units against enemy forces.[2]

Development

In-Fusio demonstrated an early mobile alpha at 3GSM in Cannes in February 2005. Stephen Palley tested it on a Motorola V525, directing individual peasants and assembling raiding parties. His preview did not identify the demo as an ExEn build.[3]
