The game

Each location has five stages. Clearing the bricks reveals a background image for the gallery. Falling bonuses change the paddle, slow the ball, add extra balls or advance to the next stage.[2]

Development

Alain Le Guirec and Eric Thommerot programmed the game. Thommerot also produced it, with art by Lionel Gischler and Axel Bertrand and music by Emmanuel Cacouault.[3]
