The game

Fight through ten levels, enemy formations and bosses. Five environments use changing palettes for day and night settings; the aircraft offer different ways to approach the enemy troops.[2]

Development

Michel Janicki programmed the game and Pierre Granier designed its levels. Simon Périn and Florian Masereel supplied artwork, with Masereel also contributing additional level design.[3]
