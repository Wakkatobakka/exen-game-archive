The game

The screen is split into nine firing zones, each mapped to a number key. Machines attack from its edges while supplies fall into view. The mission is to destroy Skynet’s time machine before a Terminator travels back to kill John Connor.[2]

Development

Eric Thommerot programmed and produced the ExEn edition. Marc Pestka designed the game, while Emmanuel Cacouault wrote its music and led testing.[3]
