The game

The fairies need their clothes in the right order before the party can begin. Classic mode has three tutorials and fifteen stages. Arcade mode continues indefinitely, but the fairies become less patient and two cancellations end the game.[2]

Development

Infraworlds developed this isometric game for In-Fusio.[1]
