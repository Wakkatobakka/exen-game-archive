The game

A gorilla has kidnapped Temu’s fiancée. He races against the clock through twenty-four levels, dodging hazards, collecting speed bonuses and riding crocodiles. The gorilla is also playable.[2]

Development

Michel Janicki programmed the game and Pascal Saingré designed its levels. Devalley artists Florian Masereel and Simon Périn created the graphics, with Emmanuel Cacouault supplying music.[3]
