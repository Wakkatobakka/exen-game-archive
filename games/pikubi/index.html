The game

Journalist Remi and his bird Pikubi face aliens across 40 stages. Enemies can merge into larger creatures if they meet. Food makes Pikubi grow, and peppers give him a fire-breathing attack.[2]

Development

Devalley’s development team combined Michael Remus’s programming, Florian Masereel’s graphics and Mathieu Thibaut’s music. In-Fusio produced and tested the release.[3]
