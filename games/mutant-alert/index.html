The game

Mutant Alert is a first-person shooter with 12 levels. The player searches for weapons and supplies while navigating the laboratory. Number keys move and turn the scientist; the centre key fires.[2]

Development

Eric Thommerot programmed the game and Joe Schultz designed its levels. Julien Duband supplied the textures, while Axel Bertrand and Lionel Gischler worked on the 2D graphics.[3]
