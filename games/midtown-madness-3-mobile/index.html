The game

Career mode gives the player two jobs: collect injured people in an ambulance, or deliver boxes in a Mini Cooper. Checkpoint races fix the route through the city; Blitz mode lets the driver choose the order.[2]

Development

BeTomorrow developed the mobile adaptation. Its ExEn team included Alexandre Ribeiro, Frédéric Dubuisson and Gwénaël Le Méro on programming, with Nicolas Villeminot on graphics. The three programmers also worked on the ExEn Tomb Raider adaptations.[3][4]
