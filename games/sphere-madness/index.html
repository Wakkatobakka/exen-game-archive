The game

The player guides a ball past holes, traps and enemies, collecting keys and pressing switches to open doors. Shops sell bonuses and power-ups between the hazards.[2]

Development

Martial Bossard worked on programming, graphics and level design, with Philippe Dessoly also contributing artwork. The sequel kept Bossard on programming and levels.[3][4]
