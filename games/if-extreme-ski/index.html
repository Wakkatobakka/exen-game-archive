The game

An on-screen sequence guides each trick. After completing one, the player can attempt another for more points or prepare to land. A retired skiing expert coaches the climb through the competition.[2]

Development

Devalley returned with several of the people credited on I.F. Ski Jumping: François-Xavier Casseron in programming, Michael Remus in project management and Florian Masereel in graphics. Masereel also designed this game with Thomas Rofidal.[3][4]
