The game

Choose a player by strength, stamina and speed, then place shots to the left or right and smash high returns. Match winnings pay for shoes, rackets and training.[2]

Development

Lionel Lemoullec programmed IF Ping Pong, with artwork by Axel Bertrand. Emmanuel Cacouault supplied both its music and beta testing for In-Fusio.[3]
