The game

Win matches to advance through twelve historical periods. Scores depend on the time taken and the number of squares held at the end. Quick games and preset challenges offer alternatives to the main progression.[2]

Development

NessProd provided the programming, Devalley the artwork, and In-Fusio the production and testing. Frédéric Nespoulous programmed the game; Sandra Dousset, Marc Manier and Florian Masereel created its art.[3]
