The game

Lara searches for Cinnabar through 15 levels of climbing, gunfights and puzzles. Inventory items and movable objects help her reach the next exit.[2]

Development

The original mobile trilogy was developed by IOMO. BeTomorrow adapted it to ExEn under In-Fusio’s licensing agreement with Eidos.[3]
