The game

Monsters are invading children’s dreams. Flynn can jump on enemies or throw playing cards at them as he crosses ten levels, spread across five fantasy environments.[2]

Development

Flynn’s Adventures adapts Nightmare Busters, a Super Nintendo project conceived in the 1990s. Christophe Gayraud and Jean-Christophe Alessandri are credited on the ExEn game for programming and graphics respectively.[2][3]

Recalling the earlier SNES project in 2024, Alessandri described working remotely with Gayraud. They exchanged work through a modem and used a PC tool to assemble sprites and maps before testing executable builds on a console.[4]
