The game

The player switches between Anakin, Obi-Wan, Mace Windu and Yoda over 12 levels. Film locations include Kashyyyk, Mustafar and Coruscant, with boss fights against characters such as Grievous and Dooku.[2]

Development

In-Fusio developed the ExEn game for THQ Wireless. Michel Janicki programmed it, with graphics by Lionel Gischler, Axel Bertrand and Simon Périn.[3]
