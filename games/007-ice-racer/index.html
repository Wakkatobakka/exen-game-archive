The game

Drive the Vanquish past obstacles and collect extra time, points and speed. One mission replaces the race against the clock with a fight against Zao, using rockets picked up on the road.[2]

Development

Marc Pestka designed the game and its levels. Programming was shared by Benoit Laffitte, Jérôme Schurch and Eric Thommerot; Schurch also produced it.[3]

Release and reception

The Q Store offered optional power-ups paid for by SMS. DJcube revisited the ExEn game in 2023 and completed its four missions in about six minutes.[2]
