The game

The boat accelerates automatically while the player steers around rivals. First place unlocks the next race.[2]

Development

Otaboo developed Malibu Ride 2 for In-Fusio. Alain Fernandes wrote the code, Philippe Dessoly created the artwork, and In-Fusio handled production and testing.[3]
