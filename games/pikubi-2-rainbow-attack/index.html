The game

The sequel keeps the platform arenas and enemies that merge when they meet. Pikubi can now fly, while bonuses provide points or a flame attack.[2]

Development

Michael Remus and Mathieu Thibaut returned on programming and music. Florian Masereel took the design credit, joined by programmer Mathieu Laissy and gameplay contributor Thomas Rofidal.[3]
