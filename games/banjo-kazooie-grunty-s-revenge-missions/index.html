The game

Grunty has trapped Kazooie inside a scrambled magic jigsaw. Banjo earns jiggies by completing tasks, including collecting eggs on a slide and fishing for passing sheep.[2]

Development

Stephen Palley played an early mobile version on a Motorola V525 at 3GSM in February 2005. His preview described ten mini-games, with five available at the start. The preview did not identify the demo as ExEn.[2]

Rare licensed its games to In-Fusio for the mobile adaptations. In a February 2005 Scribes response, Rare explained who was arranging the development:[3]

Edition and research notes

Rare’s response concerns its forthcoming mobile ports collectively. It does not name the programmers of the ExEn Missions edition.[3]

The 3GSM preview was played on a Motorola V525; the exact relationship to the ExEn package has not been established.[2]
