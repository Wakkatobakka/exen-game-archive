The game

Each sister has her own special power. The route to the exit runs through platforms, monsters and bonuses, with a time limit for each level. The three castles contain thirty levels in total.[2]

Development

DC Studios developed the game for In-Fusio. Neil Galbraith and Francis C. Bullen worked as both programmers and producers, while In-Fusio handled its own production, product management and testing.[3]
