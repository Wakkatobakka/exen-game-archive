The game

A black hole sends the pilot back to 1914. Shoot down enough enemies and defeat each boss to move into the next period. The plane fires automatically, leaving the player to steer and avoid attacks.[2]

Development

Michel Janicki programmed the ExEn game, with art by Lionel Gischler and music by Emmanuel Cacouault. Eric Thommerot produced it for In-Fusio.[3]
