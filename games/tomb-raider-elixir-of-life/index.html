The game

After recovering the Osiris Codex and Cinnabar, Lara brings both artifacts to a mysterious mansion in England. The final episode continues the trilogy’s side-scrolling platforming and puzzles.[2]

Development

IOMO created the original mobile game. BeTomorrow produced the ExEn adaptation, with In-Fusio distributing it through mobile operators.[3]
