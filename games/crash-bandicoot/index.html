The game

Crash collects 60 crystals across 20 levels, using spins, slides, jumps and crawling.[2]

Development

In-Fusio approached Kaolink in July 2002 with a brief based on the Game Boy Advance game. Development began in August, with roughly three and a half months needed for colour and monochrome versions.[3]

The team reported around 1/150th of the GBA version’s space, and controls that accepted one button at a time.[3]
