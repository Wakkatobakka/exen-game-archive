EXEN / METHODS & CREDITS

About the archive

A true archive of the game and its history, not just a file.

The collection

Wakka’s ExEn archive began with 46 games listed by My Abandonware. It brings together game descriptions, development accounts, personal credits, contemporary coverage and archival images. 35 entries currently include named contributors.

All original game-page URLs have been retained. The collection is a starting point, rather than a complete ExEn release list.

Writing and attribution

Direct quotations retain the source’s wording and name the speaker or writer. Translated quotations identify the translation. Summaries are written for this archive and link to their sources.

Credit records distinguish the ExEn adaptation from earlier games and other phone editions. The creator index contains selected roles from interviews and MobyGames credit transcriptions; it is not a complete staff directory.

Company announcements describe plans and availability at the time of publication. Later interviews are attributed recollections.

Images

Historical images have captions, source links and reproduction or contributor credits. An ExEn screenshot is identified by its source platform label. Magazine images retain their publication references. Screenshots from a different version are not substituted for the ExEn game.

Dates and original files

Catalog years refer to the ExEn entry in the source collection. Individual histories explain earlier releases or differences between editions. Crash, for example, has French magazine coverage from 2002–03 and a later US catalog listing.

No original game packages were downloaded, inspected, hashed or tested during this archive research. Original-file details stay separate from game history.

Source communities

My Abandonware provides the original collection and platform-labelled image galleries. MobyGames supplies credit records, game descriptions and contributor indexes. Crash Bandicoot Zone reproduces and translates the Mobiles Magazine material. Other credited sources include Rare Gamer, Tomb Raider Chronicles, NoRecess, Dr JVtek and Terre de Jeux.

Individual entries identify additional reviewers, artists, publishers and researchers. Game files remain at their original source links.

Corrections and additions

Send a correction or source with the game title, edition and supporting reference. Wakka reviews changes before publication.
